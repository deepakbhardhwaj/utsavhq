Everything your event business runs on

Thirteen connected modules — enter data once and it flows everywhere, from the lead to the final payment.

CRM & Leads

Lead cards, sources, status tracking, auto-assign to staff, bulk import and instant search.

Events & Functions

Multi-date events, a venue and schedule per function, event sheets and post-event feedback photos.

Sales Hub

GST invoices, rental invoices, quotations, financial-year filters and payment-health badges.

Purchase Hub

Purchase orders, vendor bills, asset purchases, payment-out entries and vendor advances.

Inventory & Godown

Item master with units and rates, stock transfer, inward/outward challans and a full audit log.

Party Ledger

Dr/Cr ledger for every client and vendor, opening balance, date filters and Tally-style format.

Bank & Cash

Cash and bank accounts, transactions, cheque clearance, deposits, withdrawals and passbook.

Expenses

Custom categories, due tracking, advance linking, sub-filters and category-wise reports.

Tasks

Task engine with quick tasks, meeting tasks, reminders and a WhatsApp sender for your team.

Reports

Profit & Loss with direct/indirect split, Balance Sheet, Day Book, sales and purchase reports.

Staff & Permissions

Role-based access per module, staff approval, and one-device session locking for security.

Multi-Company

Run several businesses side by side — each with its own data, staff and invite links.

Nexa AI Assistant

Ask about dues, events, expenses or profit in plain English or Hinglish — free, built into the app.

Mobile + Offline

Installable app for Android and iOS, fast on mobile, and it keeps working when the network drops.

Professional Prints

Clean PDF invoices, vouchers, ledgers and event sheets — ready to share on WhatsApp.
