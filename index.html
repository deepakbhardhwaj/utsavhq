Help & frequently asked questions

Everything people usually ask before getting started.

Do I need to install anything?

No. UTSAVhq runs in the browser. On mobile you can add it to your home screen in one tap and it behaves like a normal app — same on desktop.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The app is installable and keeps working offline; your entries sync back to your workspace as soon as you are online again.

Is my business data safe?

Every company gets its own separate workspace, and only the people you add can see it. You control exactly which modules each staff member can open, and each login is locked to one device.

Can my staff use it too?

Yes. Add your team, choose their role, and decide module by module what they can see and do — accounts, sales, purchases, inventory and more.

I run more than one business. Is that supported?

Yes. You can run several companies side by side, each with its own data, staff and reports, and switch between them instantly.
